With the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the European Union harmonised and strengthened its data protection law. In Switzerland, the new and strengthened Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) entered into force on 1 September 2023.

We help companies as well as individuals and organisations navigate demanding data protection law. To that end we offer flexible and affordable solutions such as our [privacy policy generator](/scan). We share our knowledge through the [Datenschutz Academy](/academy), our [newsletter](https://www.datenschutzpartner.ch/newsletter/), [our podcast](https://www.datenschutzpartner.ch/podcast/) and [social media](/contact).
